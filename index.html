Booking guide.

Everything you need to know before requesting a visit. Every rule below still needs confirmation from By.Niadania — nothing here is final policy yet.

1 · Before booking

Pick the service you want, then choose a date and time that suit you. Fill in the short form — your request goes straight to WhatsApp as a ready-made message.

2 · Confirmation

Sending the form does not reserve your slot yet. By.Niadania reviews every request personally and confirms the schedule with you in chat.

3 · Reschedule & cancellation

Plans change — that's fine. Just message us as early as you can and we'll sort out a new time together.

4 · Prices & preparation

Prices shown are drafts from the owner's material — confirm the latest price in chat. For nail art, come with bare nails if possible; prep tips for other treatments will be shared in chat.
